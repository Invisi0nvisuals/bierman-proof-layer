### Introduction

Following the delivery of the initial SEO Business Impact Audit, Bierman provided additional downstream CRM data, including a comprehensive HubSpot/CallRail reconciliation analysis (May 2025–April 2026) and a Pass 2 review of broader New Jersey Google Business Profile (GBP) performance. 

The original audit was scoped to upstream data (GSC, GA4, GTM, and agency documentation) and concluded that Bierman's primary challenge was not technical SEO URL structures, but rather conversion optimization, local intake, and attribution resolution. This new downstream evidence strongly validates that conclusion. It demonstrates that Bierman is actively engaged in sophisticated multi-touch analysis and that search is already the organization's dominant acquisition engine.

This addendum integrates these new findings with the original audit, refining the recommendations for the 2-location pilot without altering the core business-impact thesis.

---

### 1. What This New Material Adds

**The CRM Attribution Analysis**
The new HubSpot/CallRail data establishes the true top-of-funnel volume and the multi-touch reality of the client journey. Over the 12-month window, Bierman generated 30,115 real leads, resulting in 326 reconciled active clients. Crucially, the data reveals that first-touch attribution severely undercounts middle-funnel channels. For example, Email Marketing is credited with only 2 active clients on a first-touch basis, but touched 86 active clients throughout their journey—a 43× undercount.

**The GBP Pass 2 Review**
While the original audit reviewed GBP performance strictly for the pilot candidates (Ramsey and Piscataway), the Pass 2 review adds network-level context across Tier 2 New Jersey profiles (December 2025–May 2026). It reveals significant local action volume across the state, including high-interaction profiles like Berkeley Heights (703 interactions, 133 calls), Princeton (737 interactions, 116 calls), and West Orange (758 interactions, 112 calls). It also confirms that appointment URLs are generally absent and location-specific UTMs are not currently applied to GBP website links.

---

### 2. Where It Supports the Original Audit

**Search is the Dominant Engine**
The CRM data proves that search visibility is highly functional. Combined search channels (Google My Business, Organic Search, Paid Search) drove 513 all-time active clients. This definitively refutes the AI-generated hypothesis that technical URL defects are causing a catastrophic failure in organic acquisition.

**GBP is the Primary Battlefield**
The audit flagged GBP as a major source of direct action that was likely undercounted. The CRM data confirms this at scale: Google My Business is Bierman's single highest-converting channel (25.8% active rate), driving 221 active clients all-time. Furthermore, the Pass 2 review shows that GBP calls and direction requests routinely bypass the website entirely, reinforcing the need for the CallRail/HubSpot reconciliation that the audit recommended.

---

### 3. Where It Refines the Original Audit

**From "Data Missing" to "Data Integration Improving"**
The original audit noted that center-level phone attribution was unresolved. The new CRM materials show that Bierman is significantly farther along operationally than initially understood. The team has successfully retroactively reconciled 326 active clients who were previously buried under "Offline Sources," primarily by matching CallRail data. Therefore, the narrative shifts: the data is not simply missing; rather, Bierman is actively working to close structural integration gaps between CallRail and HubSpot.

**The Review-to-Demand Gap**
The Pass 2 GBP review highlights a specific conversion risk that extends beyond general reputation management. Several locations with massive interaction volume have severe review gaps:

| Location | GBP Interactions (6 mo) | Review Count | Risk Profile |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| West Orange | 758 | 1 | High visibility, near-zero trust signals |
| Princeton | 737 | 3 | High visibility, near-zero trust signals |
| Piscataway | 424 | 4 | Moderate visibility, low trust signals |

This represents a critical local trust and conversion bottleneck. Parents are finding these profiles and taking action, but the lack of reviews creates unnecessary friction before the intake call.

---

### 4. What Remains Unresolved by Location

Despite the advanced retroactive analysis, critical gaps remain that the pilot must address at the point of capture:

*   **The 6,419-Call Gap:** The CRM analysis identified 21,089 CallRail calls but only 7,085 HubSpot-tagged contacts. This 6,419-call gap confirms that the real-time integration between GTM, CallRail, and HubSpot remains leaky.
*   **Location-Specific Outcomes:** The spreadsheet provides aggregate state-level data, but it does not break down Ramsey vs. Piscataway performance at the booked-consult or patient-start level.
*   **View-Through Attribution:** The influence of Paid Social on subsequent organic searches or direct calls remains unmeasurable with current tools.

---

### 5. Updated Recommended Pilot Focus

The recommendation to execute a 2-location pilot remains unchanged, but the specific CRM tasks are updated to reflect Bierman's operational maturity.

**Tier 1 Pilot (Immediate Execution)**
*   **Ramsey (Primary):** Retained as the primary candidate. It has enough balanced activity (566 GBP interactions, 287 website clicks, 73 calls) to measure the impact of conversion rate optimization (CRO) and CTA improvements.
*   **Piscataway (Comparison):** Retained as the comparison candidate. It is direction-heavy (217 directions vs 31 calls), making it useful for testing whether current visibility is serving existing families, new families, or both.
*   **Updated Pilot Tasks:** 
    *   Initiate aggressive review generation (especially for Piscataway).
    *   Implement location-specific UTM tracking on all GBP website links.
    *   Shift the CRM objective from "begin reconciliation" to "automate and QA the current retroactive reconciliation process" so that CallRail data maps to HubSpot deals in real-time.
    *   Validate CTA and form paths to ensure location context survives the submission.

**Second-Wave Candidates (Post-Pilot)**
Based on the Pass 2 review and business context, the following locations should be prioritized for the next phase of optimization:
*   **West Orange:** Highest visibility but severe review gap.
*   **Princeton:** Strong non-branded/call activity but severe review gap.
*   **Berkeley Heights:** High call volume; ideal candidate for attribution validation.
*   **Eatontown:** Strongest visible non-branded capture; ideal for content/SEO learning.
*   **Canal Winchester:** Review-gap profile with active demand from prior analysis.
*   **Massachusetts & North Carolina:** Broader market candidates based on strategic business priorities.
